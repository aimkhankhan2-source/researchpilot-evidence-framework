# Test 1: Fu et al.

## Purpose

Test 1 was part of the initial ResearchPilot evaluation using an academic review paper related to virtual reality, autism spectrum disorder, and serious games.

## Role in Development

This test was used to evaluate the baseline ResearchPilot framework and identify weaknesses in evidence classification and traceability.

## Evaluation Focus

- paper structure extraction
- methodology analysis
- evidence identification
- limitation identification
- research-gap analysis
- future research recommendations
- evidence traceability

## Key Development Outcome

The testing contributed to identifying the need for more precise evidence provenance.

The original framework did not sufficiently distinguish between:

- findings directly reported by the active paper
- findings reported from cited studies
- AI interpretations
- calculated information
- information not reported by the paper

These observations contributed to the development of ResearchPilot V2.
