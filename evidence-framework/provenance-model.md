# Provenance Model

## Purpose

Provenance identifies where an analytical claim comes from.

## Categories

### DIRECTLY_REPORTED

The active paper reports the claim as its own information, method, result, observation, or conclusion.

### CITED_SOURCE_REPORTED

The active paper reports a finding from another cited study or source.

### CALCULATED

The claim is mathematically derived from information reported in the source.

### INFERRED

The claim is an AI interpretation or logical inference that is not explicitly stated by the authors.

### NOT_REPORTED

The information is unavailable in the active paper.

### EXTERNAL

The claim comes from an external source that was explicitly requested.

## Key Rule

Provenance answers:

> Where did this claim come from?

Provenance does not indicate whether the claim has been independently verified.
